How the estimate is calculated

The dashboard samples a grid spaced roughly 250 m apart inside the district boundary. It uses the 2021 ESA WorldCover cropland class as a baseline and takes the latest usable Sentinel‑2 observation at each sample in the last 30 days, testing up to five scenes per tile. Samples must have a vegetation or bare-soil scene classification. An NDVI of 0.35 or above is counted as green cropland. The area of those samples is added in hectares.

This pilot measures greenness on historically mapped cropland. It can miss new fields, crops early in their growth, harvested fields and small plots. Weeds can also appear green. Crop types and field-level accuracy have yet to be validated. Low coverage leaves part of the baseline unmeasured.
